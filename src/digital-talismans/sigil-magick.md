# SIGIL MAGICK

<p class="text">One intention. One hand-drawn sigil. One fire rite.</p>

I make each sigil by hand for the intention you send me. I draw it in ink, photograph it, then burn the original in an old tobacco tin.

This is a small, focused working. You might have a career move in mind, something difficult you want to see through, or a new venture you'd like to begin with a blessing. Start with one thing you can put into a sentence. If there are several issues tangled together, a larger working gives me room to address them.

## What I do

I hold your intention in mind and go into a light trance. Then I take up my fountain pen and let the lines come. This is automatic drawing. As the lines take shape, I work your intention into the geometry of the sigil.

I photograph the finished drawing. Then I burn the original in the tin. In the language of the rite, that's the moment I cast your intention into the ether. The photograph is yours to keep.

## How it works

1. **Send me your intention.** Reply to the order confirmation email with the one outcome you'd like the working to focus on.
2. **I confirm and carry out the rite.** I draw your sigil, photograph it and burn the original.
3. **I send you the photograph and instructions.** You'll receive both by email or messenger.

## What you do

Spend a few quiet moments looking at the sigil. Close your eyes and let the shape fill your inner vision. Then let it go and get on with your day. You can come back to the image whenever you feel drawn to it.

## How I charge my own sigils

> When I make a sigil for myself, I start with a precise intention. Once I've put it into a shape, I let it go.
>
> The theory is that an image can carry an intention past the part of the mind that wants to keep talking it over. I think of it as handing the intention to the chthonic part of the mind, where it can run in the background.
>
> The old language says it's working in the ether. I like both ways of putting it. Either way, I close the tin and get on with the day.
