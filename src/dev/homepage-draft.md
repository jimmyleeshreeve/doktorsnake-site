## Who the hell is Doktor Snake?

I'm Jimmy Lee Shreeve, author of *Doktor Snake's Voodoo Spellbook*, *Blood Rites* and *Cannibals*. My journalism has appeared in publications including *The Independent*, *The Daily Telegraph* and the *Financial Times*.

I explore magick, consciousness and unusual phenomena, from old folklore and forgotten landscapes to AI and the future of mind.
