## WHO THE HELL IS DOKTOR SNAKE?

I've been exploring the unusual for most of my life.

Long before artificial intelligence started
rewriting the possibilities of creativity,
I was investigating magick, folklore,
human consciousness and the hidden
structures of belief.

My work has taken me from national newspaper
journalism and mainstream book publishing
to the outer edges of the occult underground.

Along the way I've written books,
investigated strange phenomena,
worked with people facing extraordinary situations
and developed my own approaches to
attention, observation and personal agency.

Among my books:

- *Doktor Snake's Voodoo Spellbook* — St. Martin's Press
- *Blood Rites* — Random House
- *Cannibals* — John Blake

My journalism has appeared in national publications
including The Independent, Daily Telegraph
and Financial Times.

    <p>
      MachineKind explores the new world forming around

      between human and machine intelligence.
      <a href="/machinekind/">Enter MachineKind →</a>

    <summary>Do you still practise magick?</summary>
    <p>
      Yes. Magick, ritual, symbolism and the occult remain
      important parts of my work. Private workings
      and related services are available.
      <a href="/influence/">Explore Workings →</a>
    </p>
  </details>

  <details>
    <summary>What can a working be used for?</summary>
    <p>
      Clients commonly enquire about money, relationships,
      obstacles, protection and difficult transitions.
      Each situation is considered individually.
      Ritual work does not guarantee a particular outcome
      or replace necessary practical action.
    </p>
  </details>

  <details>
    <summary>How soon do you begin a working?</summary>
    <p>
      Workings are generally started within 24–48 hours
      of an accepted booking, subject to availability
      and the requirements of the operation.
    </p>
  </details>

  <details>
    <summary>How will I know a working is complete?</summary>
    <p>
      You'll receive a written Field Report or
      other agreed completion material.
    </p>
  </details>

  <details>
    <summary>Do I have to believe in magick?</summary>
    <p>
      No. The writing, MachineKind research and Dokology
      mentorship can be approached without supernatural beliefs.
      Symbolic methods can also be explored as exercises
      in attention, creativity and intention.
    </p>

  <details>
    <summary>Are private enquiries confidential?</summary>
