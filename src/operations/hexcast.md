### The Albatross

The albatross, a large, majestic seabird, was once deeply feared and
revered by sailors, who considered it a bird of ill-omen. With its
enormous wingspan and ability to glide effortlessly over vast oceans,
the albatross often followed ships for days on end, almost as if it were
observing the crew's every move.

Killing an albatross was thought to bring disaster and misfortune to a
ship, dooming its voyage and cursing its crew. This superstition was
immortalized in Samuel Taylor Coleridge's *The Rime of the Ancient
Mariner*, where a sailor's act of shooting an albatross brings terrible
curses upon the ship, symbolizing the delicate balance between Earth
bipeds and the forces of nature.
