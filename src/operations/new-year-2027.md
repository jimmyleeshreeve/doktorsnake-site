## The landscape: where the wild meets the ordered world

<img class="about-hero" src="/assets/img/st-marys-church-santon-downham.jpg" alt="St Mary's Church at Santon Downham, illuminated at dusk among dark trees" loading="lazy">

At the edge of Santon Downham stands St Mary's Church, with stonework dating back to the twelfth century. Beside the village, the forest begins. The church occupies a striking threshold between human settlement and the older, less predictable world of the woods.

Above its south doorway is an enigmatic Norman carving, sometimes interpreted as a wolf and the Tree of Life. I think of it as the **Guardian Beast** — my own name for a figure whose precise meaning remains uncertain.

This landscape knows what it means for Chaos to intrude upon Order. Centuries ago, windblown sand overwhelmed parts of Santon Downham. Yet the village endured.

And farther along the forest road near Brandon stands an ancient burial mound.

## The two guardians

<img class="about-hero" src="/assets/img/mound-brandon.jpg" alt="Grass-covered prehistoric burial mound in woodland near Brandon, Suffolk" loading="lazy">

Years ago, I was passing this mound when I saw something I have never forgotten.

**Two deer stood together on its summit. One pure white. One pure black.**

Both looked directly at me. Neither moved. Neither flinched.

Perhaps they were simply curious. Perhaps they were enjoying giving Doktor Snake something to think about. Either way, the encounter stayed with me.

For this New Year working, the two deer become the symbolic **Guardians of the Balance**: White and Black. Stillness and Motion. The known and the unknown. And between them, the Crystalline Pinpoint.
