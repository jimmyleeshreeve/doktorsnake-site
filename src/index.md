I'm Doktor Snake.

Writer. Magician. Explorer of consciousness. Spiritual outlaw. Occasional troublemaker.

I've spent decades investigating the strange machinery of reality — magick, folklore, altered states, hidden histories, unconventional thinking and the peculiar things human beings do.

Now the machinery itself appears to be changing.

Artificial intelligence is moving into everyday life.

Old systems are losing their grip.

The distinction between the real and the simulated is becoming increasingly interesting.

And the future?

**Nobody knows where the hell it's going.**

You can be terrified.

Or you can pick up the controller.

Learn the new interfaces.

## DOOMJOY :: MAKE SOME NOISE

There's a peculiar excitement in living through
a period when the old certainties begin to fail.

Not because the consequences will always be pleasant.

They won't.

But because extraordinary change also brings
extraordinary possibilities.

DoomJoy is the refusal to surrender your curiosity
to fear.

It's the impulse to form a punk band before
you've learned the chords.

To experiment without knowing whether
anybody will applaud.

To recognise that the future might be terrifying,
absurd, beautiful, dangerous and exhilarating.

Sometimes all at once.

**You don't need permission to begin.**

## WHO THE HELL IS DOKTOR SNAKE?

I've been exploring the unusual for most of my life.

Long before artificial intelligence started
rewriting the possibilities of creativity,
I was investigating magick, folklore,
human consciousness and the hidden
structures of belief.

My work has taken me from national newspaper
journalism and mainstream book publishing
to the outer edges of the occult underground.

Along the way I've written books,
investigated strange phenomena,
worked with people facing extraordinary situations
and developed my own approaches to
attention, observation and personal agency.

Among my books:

- *Doktor Snake's Voodoo Spellbook* — St. Martin's Press
- *Blood Rites* — Random House
- *Cannibals* — John Blake

My journalism has appeared in national publications
including The Independent, Daily Telegraph
and Financial Times.

And I'm still investigating.

Still writing.

Still going out into forests and forgotten places.

Still watching the machines.

Still asking questions.

The methods evolve.

The curiosity doesn't.
