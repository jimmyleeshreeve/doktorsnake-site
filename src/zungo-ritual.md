> *Insane shouting. Harrowing screams. Soul-chilling chants amid dancing obsidian flames.*

That was how outsiders described some of the old rites.

They heard noise.

They saw fire.

They saw people they didn't understand calling upon powers they didn't recognise.

But underneath the lurid descriptions was something much older and more interesting:

**the magick of people who possessed very little except themselves.**

No elaborate temples.

No authorised scriptures.

No permission.

Fragments survived as charms, spoken formulas, songs, curses, blessings, spirit work, mantras, wanga and whispered instructions passed from one person to another.

Different cultures gave these things different names.

Zungo belongs to that territory.

Not historical reconstruction.

Not religion.

A living nightside working built from fragments, correspondences, folklore and field practice — adapted for the world we find ourselves living in now.
