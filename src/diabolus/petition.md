There is an old idea that the Devil grants wishes.

Not necessarily because he is evil.

More because he belongs to that older category of beings who operate at the edge of the permitted world.

The crossroads.

The forest.

The solitary stone.

The place outside ordinary rules.

The figure who says:

> **You have been told there is no way forward. Let us examine that claim.**

This is the Lord Satan that interests me.

Not the cartoon villain with horns and pitchfork.

The **Adversary**.

The **Trickster**.

The bringer of forbidden knowledge.

The Promethean intelligence that challenges imposed limits and asks whether the barriers surrounding you are actually real — or whether you have simply accepted them as such.

## THE OLD CURRENT

England has a long history of people turning to Old Nick when ordinary methods appeared exhausted.

In folklore, those seeking aid might circle a church, ancient stone or lonely place nine times, calling upon the Devil until he appeared and heard their request.

Whether taken as supernatural history, folklore or psychological technology, the pattern remains fascinating.

You leave the ordinary world.

You state what you want.

You confront whatever stands between you and it.

And then you return.

Changed, ideally, in some useful way.

## ONE WISH

Your petition may concern:

**Money. Business. Opportunity. Love. Protection. Luck. Influence. Empowerment.**

Or something more difficult to categorise.

Something blocked.

Something that has resisted ordinary efforts.

Something you have decided must change.

You give me **one primary intention**.

I take it into the working.

## THE DEVIL'S PUNCHBOWL

In an ancient woodland in South Norfolk, beneath oak and yew, lies a secluded pool traditionally known as the **Devil's Punchbowl**.

At the water's edge I conduct the working personally.

I raise my hands, palms upward, and address Lord Satan not as a servant or beggar, but as one approaching the primordial current of rebellion, intelligence and Promethean fire.

> "Lord Satan, I petition you not as a servant nor beggar, but as one who seeks truth. I stand here open to your wisdom and Promethean fire."

Then your intention is spoken.

> "On behalf of our dear friend, I ask you to cast this request into the burning fires of creation, making it come to pass in the realm of the Matterium."

The working is recorded and delivered privately as an audiovisual transmission.

But that is not necessarily where the operation ends.

## THE WORKING IS ONLY THE BEGINNING

Sometimes the Devil doesn't hand you what you asked for.

Sometimes he points at the door you somehow failed to notice.

And grins.

A recent petition began with a business objective.

The ritual established the intention.

What followed was something much more extensive:

Research.

Market analysis.

Financial modelling.

Operational strategy.

Custom software.

Brand development.

Print-ready business materials.

The petition did not replace action.

It established the **vector**.

Everything afterwards involved discovering and constructing the routes through which the intention might actually enter the Matterium.

That is increasingly how I understand these workings.

**Majik establishes direction.**

**Attention reveals openings.**

**Action creates consequences.**

You may receive observations or practical suggestions arising from the working where they are relevant.

Not because Satan is a celestial management consultant.

Because sometimes stating exactly what you intend to change makes previously invisible possibilities much easier to see — and act upon — increasing the likelihood that your desired outcome will come to pass in the Matterium.
