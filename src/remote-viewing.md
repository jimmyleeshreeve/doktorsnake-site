# REMOTE VIEWING

<p class="text">One target. Enter the void. See what comes.</p>

Some situations resist ordinary analysis.

You can go over the facts, replay conversations and consider every possibility. Yet something remains out of reach. You sense there is more to the situation, but you can’t quite see it.

**Remote Viewing is a way of approaching that unknown.**

You give me a target: a situation, a place, an event or a question you want to explore. I enter a receptive state, focus on the target and record the impressions that arise.

These may come as images, sensations, fragments, symbols or an unexpected sense of what is taking place. Afterwards, I look at the material as a whole and give you my interpretation.

**What comes, comes.**

## How it works

### 1. Set the target

After placing your order, reply to the confirmation email with the situation or question you would like me to focus on. Include any background you feel is relevant. If we have already discussed your situation, simply let me know if there is anything you would like to add.

We keep the focus to **one situation or question**, so the session has a clear target. This could involve a relationship, a career situation, a decision, a place or something else that has been on your mind.

### 2. Enter the void

I set aside ordinary analysis and move into a quiet, receptive state. With the target in mind, I allow impressions to emerge and record them as they come.

### 3. Record and interpret

After the session, I review what came through. I separate the impressions themselves from my interpretation, so you can see both the raw material and the meaning I draw from it.

You receive a written report by email, typically within 48 hours of my receiving your reply.

## What you receive

- One focused Remote Viewing session.
- A written account of the images, sensations and other impressions that arose.
- My interpretation of the session and how it may relate to your target.
- One follow-up question by email about the session.

## Seeing through the veil

We experience reality through images, perceptions and interpretations. Whether you see the world as a simulation, a mystery, or simply something we don’t yet fully understand, there may be more to a situation than is immediately visible.

Remote Viewing is an attempt to look beyond the surface.

I don’t claim that every impression will be literal, verifiable or correct. Some may be symbolic; others may turn out to have no clear meaning. A session is an exploration, not proof of what another person is thinking or doing.

The aim is to see what emerges—and consider it with an open mind.
