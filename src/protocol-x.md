## When Ordinary Measures Have Failed, Escalate the Signal

Protocol X is a standalone maximum-intensity working.

It is not Diabolus. It does not require allegiance to a particular deity, demon, spirit or religious system.

And unlike workings built around multiple requests, Protocol X is deliberately restricted to **one objective**.

One situation.

One desired outcome.

Everything is concentrated upon it.

The principle is simple: when the stakes are high, don't divide the signal.

## The Hollow Hill

The central working is conducted in the field at one of the ancient mounds of the East Anglian landscape.

Such places have accumulated stories for centuries.

Burial mounds became fairy hills. Ancient earthworks became entrances to another world. Folklore spoke of the **Hollow Hills** — places where the familiar landscape touched something older and stranger.

Protocol X does not require you to believe any of this literally.

The Hollow Hill serves as the terrestrial anchor: ancient ground, accumulated human presence, and a physical location removed from the noise of ordinary life.

This is where the main working begins.

## Above The Hill

There is another element.

Far above the landscape lie regions of gravitational equilibrium created by the Earth and Moon.

Associated with the L4 and L5 Lagrange regions are the extraordinarily faint **Kordylewski dust clouds** — concentrations of material occupying an unusual balance between larger forces.

Protocol X uses this as its upper symbolic architecture.

**The Hollow Hill below.**

**The equilibrium field above.**

**The human objective between them.**

The purpose is not to invoke something from the sky.

It is to establish the objective within a symbolic system built around balance, concentration and the redirection of forces.

Then the signal is reinforced.

## Seven Days

Protocol X unfolds as a seven-day working.

The first stage is **Target Lock**.

Your situation is reduced to a single clear objective. Ambiguity is stripped away. The target has to be something that can be stated cleanly.

The principal Hollow Hill working is then conducted in the field.

Across the following days, I carry out **three reinforcement transmissions**, maintaining concentration on the same objective rather than introducing additional requests.

The final stage closes the operation and releases the objective.

No additional intentions are introduced during the sequence.

**One target remains one target.**

## Your Private Signal Artefact

Protocol X also produces something unique to the operation.

You receive a private video signal artefact created from imagery and material associated with your working.

It functions as the talisman of the operation — something you can keep on your phone or computer and return to when you want to reconnect with the objective.

It is created for your working and is not published or reused.

## What Protocol X Is For

Protocol X is intended for significant situations where you want to bring sustained attention to one outcome.

That might involve money, business, career, relationships, personal direction, an entrenched situation, or a major change you are attempting to bring about.

It is deliberately not designed as a list of wishes.

If there are five unrelated things you want changed, another working may be more appropriate.

Protocol X asks a different question:

**What is the one thing that matters most?**

Everything follows from there.

## No Belief Required

You do not need to share my worldview.

You do not need to believe in magick, spirits, ancient gods, faeries, Kordylewski Clouds as intelligences, or anything else.

Protocol X can equally be understood as ritual, concentrated intention, psychogeographic fieldwork, symbolic engineering, or something stranger.

The working remains the same.

**One objective. Maximum concentration. Seven-day working.**
